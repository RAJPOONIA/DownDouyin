Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Which video and photo platforms are supported by DownDouyin?

DownDouyin supports four major platforms: Douyin (抖音), Bilibili (哔哩哔哩 / B站), TikTok, and Xiaohongshu (小红书 / RedNote). You can paste either a direct video URL, a short share URL (such as v.douyin.com, b23.tv, vm.tiktok.com, or xhslink.com), or the full share text copied from the mobile app.

2. How do I fetch and download a watermark-free video?

Copy the video link from the source app, paste it into the search input box at the top of DownDouyin, and click Fetch Video. Once the Video Preview card loads, review the available quality rows (such as Original Quality, 1080p Full HD, or 720p HD) and click Download next to your preferred option.

3. How do the Video, Audio, and Thumbnail tabs work?

Inside the Video Preview card, three format tabs appear based on what the source platform provides for that post:

  • Video: Lists all extracted video streams with resolution, format (MP4), and file size.
  • Audio: Extracts the original background soundtrack or audio stream (saved as MP3) when available.
  • Thumbnail: Lets you download the high-resolution cover image (JPG). If the post is a multi-photo album or Live Photo gallery, a full Photo Album grid and Download All (ZIP) button also appear below the preview.

4. Why do some videos show more quality options than others?

Available resolutions depend on the original file uploaded by the creator and the streams exposed by the platform. For example, if a creator uploaded a 720p video, 1080p or 4K streams will not exist. On Bilibili, guest requests typically return up to 720p/1080p; configuring your optional Bilibili SESSDATA cookie in Settings unlocks 1080p60 and 4K UHD streams when available on the source video.

5. Why can a large Original Quality download pause or take longer?

Original Quality master files (often 50 MB to 200+ MB) are streamed from upstream CDN servers in China or overseas. Occasionally a specific CDN edge node may throttle bandwidth mid-transfer. DownDouyin automatically probes multiple CDN mirrors, displays real-time percentage progress, and uses HTTP Range resume to recover from mid-stream stalls without restarting from 0%.

6. Why did my Xiaohongshu or Douyin link fail to parse?

Common reasons include:

  • Missing xsec_token on Xiaohongshu: Xiaohongshu requires the security token attached to fresh share links. Copy a fresh link directly from the Xiaohongshu app or web address bar without stripping query parameters.
  • Private, deleted, or region-restricted posts: DownDouyin can only parse publicly accessible posts. Videos set to "Friends Only" or deleted by the author cannot be fetched.

7. Is DownDouyin free to use, and do I need an account?

Yes. DownDouyin is 100% free to use and requires no account registration or subscription.

8. What are your privacy and copyright rules?

DownDouyin does not store copies of downloaded videos on its servers; media streams are proxied directly to your browser. Users are responsible for respecting creator copyrights and platform terms—only download content for personal offline viewing or when you have permission from the rights holder. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for full details.

Complete Step-by-Step Download Guide

Follow this step-by-step guide to copy links accurately from each platform, preview streams, and save watermark-free videos, audio tracks, and photo albums.

Step 1: How to Copy a Video Link from Douyin (抖音)

Open the Douyin app or website, tap the Share (分享) arrow icon on the right side of the video, and select Copy Link (复制链接). You can paste the entire share sentence (e.g., 7.43 复制打开抖音... https://v.douyin.com/xxxxx/) directly into DownDouyin—you do not need to manually edit out the Chinese text.

Step 2: How to Copy a Video Link from Bilibili (哔哩哔哩)

On the Bilibili mobile app, tap the three-dot menu or Share button and tap Copy Link (which gives a https://b23.tv/... link). On desktop web, copy the browser URL containing the BV... ID (e.g., https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1xxxxxx). For multi-part videos, a page selector dropdown (P1, P2...) will appear automatically in the Video Preview header.

Step 3: How to Copy a Video Link from TikTok

In the TikTok app or web player, tap Share → Copy Link. Both short links (https://vm.tiktok.com/... or https://vt.tiktok.com/...) and full desktop links (https://www.tiktok.com/@creator/video/123456...) are supported.

Step 4: How to Copy a Share Link from Xiaohongshu (小红书 / RedNote)

Open the Xiaohongshu note, tap the Share icon in the top-right corner, and tap Copy Link (复制链接). Important: Always use a fresh share link or full browser URL that includes the xsec_token=... parameter, as Xiaohongshu blocks bare URLs without a valid security token.

Step 5: Paste the Link and Click "Fetch Video"

Paste your copied link into the input bar at the top of DownDouyin. The platform indicator button (Douyin, Bilibili, TikTok, or Xiaohongshu) will automatically highlight the detected platform. Click Fetch Video (or press Enter) and wait 1–3 seconds for metadata extraction.

Step 6: Switch Between Video, Audio, and Thumbnail Tabs

Once the Video Preview card loads, you can click the play button on the thumbnail to preview the video, or switch between:

  • Video Tab: View all available video resolutions, codecs, and file sizes.
  • Audio Tab: Download the standalone audio/music track in MP3 format.
  • Thumbnail Tab: Download the original high-definition cover image in JPG format.

Step 7: Select Quality and Click Download

Click any quality row (for example, Original Quality for the highest bitrate master stream, or 720p HD for a smaller file size) and click the purple ⬇ Download button. The button will display live download percentage progress (0% → 99% → ✓ Saved) and save the file to your device's Downloads folder without navigating away from the page.

Step 8: Troubleshooting Failed or Slow Downloads

  • Link fails to parse: Verify the video is public and paste a fresh share link from the app.
  • Download stalls on a huge 4K/Original video: Keep the browser tab open for a few seconds—DownDouyin automatically switches to backup CDN mirrors and resumes from the exact byte offset. You can also try selecting the 1080p or 720p row if your local connection is slow.

DownDouyin Guides & Technical Articles

Quality & Codecs

Understanding Original Quality vs. 1080p & H.265/HEVC Streams

When you fetch a Douyin or Bilibili video on DownDouyin, you will often see Original Quality alongside 1080p Full HD, 720p HD, and codec labels such as H.264 or H.265.

Original Quality represents the highest-bitrate master stream returned by the platform's API without re-encoding or watermark overlays. Lower tiers (such as 720p or 540p) are smaller transcoded versions ideal for saving mobile data or sharing on messaging apps. If two quality rows share the same resolution (e.g., 480p or 1080p), DownDouyin displays their codec and bitrate (in kbps) so you can choose between universal compatibility (H.264) and higher compression efficiency (H.265/AV1).

Platform Tips

Why Xiaohongshu & Short Share Links Expire (And How to Fix Parse Errors)

Short share URLs from Douyin (v.douyin.com), TikTok (vm.tiktok.com), Bilibili (b23.tv), and Xiaohongshu (xhslink.com) redirect to full URLs containing temporary tokens. In particular, Xiaohongshu enforces a dynamic xsec_token query parameter on web notes.

If an older bookmarked URL returns a parse error, open the post in the official app, tap Share → Copy Link to generate a fresh token, and paste the full share text directly into DownDouyin.

Multi-Image & Live Photos

Downloading Douyin & Xiaohongshu Photo Albums and Live Photos

Many posts on Douyin and Xiaohongshu are multi-image carousels or Live Photo (实况动态) albums rather than single videos. When DownDouyin detects an image album, it automatically displays the Photo Album gallery below the preview card.

You can download individual high-resolution JPG images, save individual Live Photo MP4 motion clips, or click ⬇ Download All (ZIP) to bundle the entire album into a single ZIP archive.

Streaming Architecture

How DownDouyin Protects Your Privacy & Streams Media Without Server Storage

Unlike services that queue your video on remote cloud disks, DownDouyin resolves media metadata in real time and streams video bytes directly through an unbuffered proxy with automatic CDN failover. Downloaded videos and photo albums are never stored permanently on our servers.
